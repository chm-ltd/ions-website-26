Biography • Heritage

A Life Dedicated to Sacred Sound

✠

With decades of experience seated at the console of fine cathedral, collegiate, and parish instruments, Christopher Myhill brings musical sensitivity and liturgical understanding to every occasion.

Formed in the distinguished English cathedral choral tradition, his musicianship balances technical precision with deep reverence. Whether guiding a choir through Renaissance polyphony, leading congregations in traditional hymnody, or inspiring private students, Christopher's mission is to keep the majesty of sacred organ repertoire alive.

Holding advanced diplomas in organ performance and choral conducting, Christopher is a Fellow of the Royal College of Organists (FRCO) and regular guest recitalist throughout the United Kingdom.
